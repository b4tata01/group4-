Institution: Definition and Usage

An Institution is defined as an established organization or system.

Key Data & Structure:

  • **Phonetics:** `[ˌɪn.stɪˈtjuː.ʃən]`
  • **Part of Speech:** Noun (n)
  • **Core Meaning:** An established organization
  • **Word Family:** institutional (adj)
  • **Collocations:** educational institution / financial institution
  • **Example Sentence:** Oxford is a famous educational institution.

Statement: Definition and Usage

A Statement is a clear, definitive expression or declaration of something, asserting a fact, an opinion, or a formal claim.

Key Data & Structure:

Reference: Definition and Usage

A Reference is the act of mentioning, citing, or consulting a source of information.

Key Data & Structure:

  • **Phonetics:** `[ˈref.ər.əns]`
  • **Part of Speech:** Noun (n)
  • **Core Meaning:** Mention or source of information
  • **Word Family:** refer (v)
  • **Collocations:** make reference to / reference book
  • **Example Sentence:** He made a reference to Shakespeare.
